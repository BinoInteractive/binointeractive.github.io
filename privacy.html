Privacy Policy — Tesselite

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

At Bino Interactive, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you play Tesselite on Android.

1. Offline-First Design & No Developer Servers

Tesselite is an offline-first single-player game. Bino Interactive does not operate custom backend servers to collect, store, or sell your personal data, nor do we embed third-party behavioral analytics trackers.

2. Local Device Storage

Your game progress—including scores, active runs, prestige upgrades, unlocked relics, cosmetics, daily streak status, and accessibility or audio settings—is stored locally on your device. You can erase local progress anytime in Settings via Delete Save Data or by clearing app storage in Android settings.

3. Optional Google Play Games Services (Cloud Saves)

On Android, you may optionally sign in with Google Play Games Services to enable automatic cloud saves across devices. If you opt in, Tesselite accesses your Google Play Games Player ID and Display Name to manage your account session and saves your game progress (Gems, Orbs, unlocked skins and backgrounds, high scores, total lines cleared, and achievements) to Google Play Games Saved Games. Signing in is completely optional—you can play 100% offline, sign out anytime in Settings, or manage and delete your Play Games cloud data through your Google Account settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tesselite integrates Google AdMob to display bottom banner ads and optional rewarded video ads (such as reviving a run or claiming daily bonus Gems). AdMob may automatically collect and process device information—including the Android Advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device diagnostics, and ad interaction data—for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing & RevenueCat)

Tesselite integrates RevenueCat and Google Play Billing to support optional in-app purchases when offered in the Shop. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user identifier and communicates with Google Play to validate purchase entitlements. All payment transactions are handled directly by Google Play; Bino Interactive never receives or stores your credit card or billing details.

6. Children's Privacy

Tesselite is intended for a general audience and Bino Interactive does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your region). Third-party services integrated into the app (Google AdMob, Google Play Games, and RevenueCat) operate under their respective privacy policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

7. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You remain in control of your data: you may delete local save data inside Settings, sign out of Google Play Games in Settings, delete Play Games cloud data via Google Play Games or Google Account settings, or manage ad preferences in Android system settings.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Tesselite evolves, such as when adding new platform features or updating SDKs. Any revisions will be reflected in-app and on this page with an updated Effective Date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact Bino Interactive at binointeractive@gmail.com or via our developer support contact listed on the Google Play Store.
